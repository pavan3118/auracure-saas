# Phase 4 — pharmacy, laboratory, invoicing and cash review

Phase 4 implements A04/A05/A06/A12 from the approved specification and the explicit Phase 4 decisions. The user approved all three billing/pharmacy/lab/cash questions before these workflows were exposed. See PHASE4-CONTRACT.md for the concrete API and state contract. Earlier phase reviews describe their historical state; their payment/result/dispensing deferrals are fulfilled here. Phase 5 is not started.

## Delivered behavior

- OWNER/RECEPTIONIST create and edit multi-line INR invoice drafts with manual prices and explicit tax percentages. BigInt calculation applies half-up rounding per line at the paise boundary and rejects unsupported precision/overflow. Issuance atomically assigns a tenant-sequential INV number and freezes the lines. Zero-total invoices are PAID. OWNER can void only unpaid draft/issued invoices with a reason and fresh replay-protected MFA.
- RECEPTIONIST records money already collected through CASH or a manually recorded UPI/CARD transaction reference. This is not a payment gateway charge or a declaration of tax compliance. Receipts cannot exceed the balance; immutable payments and invoice paid totals commit together. Tenant/method/reference uniqueness and durable request fingerprints prevent double posting.
- CASH receipts require the receptionist's own OPEN shift and atomically append a cash movement plus receipt total. Closing uses opening float + recorded cash receipts, counted cash, calculated variance and a mandatory explanation for any difference. Closing competes on the same session version as receipt recording. Closed shifts accept no new receipts; OWNER reviews with a note and fresh MFA, preserving the original ledger. A new shift can then be opened independently.
- Verified PHARMACIST accounts receive exact medicine/strength/route/unit batches with quantity, batch number and expiry date. Quantity adjustments are versioned, reasoned, appended to history and cannot make stock negative. Metadata is immutable. Expiry uses the Asia/Kolkata calendar; stock remains eligible through its stated expiry date.
- Full dispensing selects the latest applicable signed prescription and requires positive integer quantities for every line. Exact product matching and automatic FEFO allocate by expiry then batch ID. Expired stock is excluded. All line allocations, stock deductions, encounter lock, dispense record and audit commit together or roll back together. One dispense per encounter is durable across retry-ledger expiry. The UI retains the actual batch allocation snapshots.
- Generic requests capture a fixed exact alternative and reason. The prescribing DOCTOR sees the original alongside it and approves using their current password. Strength/route changes require a new prescribing revision rather than implicit dose conversion. Approval of another doctor's request, unapproved alternatives, superseded prescriptions and later prescribing revisions after dispensing are rejected. No drug-equivalence, catalogue or clinical safety recommendation is supplied.
- LAB collects samples, records manual analyte names/values/units/reference intervals/comments, edits current unsigned drafts and signs with the existing separate six-digit diagnostic PIN. Signing freezes content and records actor/time/hash. Only the requesting DOCTOR can verify that exact revision with their current password. Only LAB explicitly releases a verified current revision. OWNER and reception gain no clinical-result access.
- Corrections append a reasoned result revision and repeat technician sign-off, requesting-doctor verification and LAB release. Older signed/released records remain visible to authorized staff as retained history. Patient reads allow only their linked current RELEASED revision; beginning a correction withholds the previous release while the replacement is under review. No clinical interpretation or critical-value automation is introduced.

## Access and operational controls

All tenant scope comes from the verified JWT. Strict contracts reject tenant_id and unknown fields. PATIENT invoice/receipt/result reads additionally require their linked profile; drafts and unreleased results are hidden. Clinical verification binds to the requesting/prescribing DOCTOR rather than any doctor in the tenant. Pharmacy mutations verify current licence status inside the transaction. Password/PIN proof checks lock credential versions/hashes against concurrent changes. Five failed sensitive proofs per user/kind per 15 minutes are enforced in Mongo; successful proofs do not consume the failure budget. Owner financial proof routes have a ten-attempt/15-minute limit and consume the existing TOTP/recovery proof.

New collections have required tenant_id and additive indexes. Existing OPEN floats and ORDERED lab records without Phase 4 fields are supported as version zero/zero receipts without destructive migration. The shared 24-hour transaction retry ledger stores fingerprints/resource references, never clinical bodies or proofs; monetary creation/receipt/dispense resources carry durable unique references as well. Audit actions contain identifiers and outcomes only. Live queue payloads remain minimal and single-process as previously documented.

Workspaces now include pharmacy, lab, doctor generic approvals/result verification, invoices/manual receipts and cash close/review. Patients can be searched for invoice selection. Lists are bounded: latest 100 invoices/orders/substitutions, earliest 100 stock batches, latest applicable records within the latest 100 signed prescriptions; patient search retains cursor pagination. FEFO is limited to 100 allocation entries per dispense and fails atomically if that limit is insufficient. No silent partial dispensing is performed.
